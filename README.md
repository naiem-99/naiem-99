# Md Naiem Hossain

**Research Officer & Bioinformatician** · [icddr,b](https://www.icddrb.org/) · Dhaka, Bangladesh

I analyse genomic and transcriptomic data to answer questions in infectious disease and cancer biology. My work at icddr,b centres on *Vibrio cholerae*, vibriophages and the gut microbiome; alongside it I build reproducible single-cell and spatial transcriptomics analyses of the tumour microenvironment.

## Research focus

- **Microbial genomics & transcriptomics** — *Vibrio cholerae* O1, vibriophages, gut microbiome, metagenomics
- **Single-cell & spatial omics** — scRNA-seq, bulk RNA-seq, tumour microenvironment, copy-number inference
- **Diagnostics & vaccinology** — evaluation of cholera rapid diagnostic tests and PCR assays
